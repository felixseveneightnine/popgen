How to Use This Tool

Mission Settings

The left panel sets the mission-wide basics: name, starting money, respawn wave time, difficulty and map. Picking a map reads its .bsp for wave-relay names and tank paths, so Wave Start/Finished Relay show what was detected as a placeholder -- type your own to override it. The Sentry Buster and Advanced fields map straight to their .pop settings.

Waves & Support

The + at the left of the tab bar adds a Wave; the + Support on the right adds a support Mission (Sniper, Spy, Engineer, Sentry Buster...). Each Wave holds one or more WaveSpawns -- its own tab row just below -- and each WaveSpawn is one group of robots that spawns together under its own timing and currency.

Picking Robots

Browse the tabs on the left of the editor (Common, Minigiants, Giant, Boss, Support, Gatebot) and click or drag a robot onto a slot. Turn on Squad to fill a WaveSpawn with several robots at once -- Medics only appear in the list once Squad is on, since they only matter paired with someone to heal. A Tank works differently: drop one in and it takes over the whole WaveSpawn (only offered on maps with a tank path).

Robot Options

Each filled slot shows a Skill dropdown and an Always Crit toggle. Giants keep the skill their template sets (shown but locked); a template that already always crits shows the toggle checked and locked too, since neither can be overridden.

Wave Bar Overview

The Waves button top-right opens a drawer with a bar per wave -- currency, robot counts, and any active support -- so you can sanity-check the whole mission's pacing. Click a bar to jump straight to that wave.

Import & Random Mission

Import Popfile loads an existing .pop back in for editing, recovering settings, waves, missions and even hand-written robots it doesn't already know. Random Mission generates a fresh 6-7 wave mission with escalating difficulty, currency and robot variety -- a starting point to tweak rather than a final file.

Exporting

Download Popfile at the bottom of the sidebar writes out the finished .pop, named to match the standard mvm_<map>_<difficulty>_<name>.pop convention.
